The 16th Centre of the Federal Security Service (FSB) of the Russian Federation has been observed operating the Turla intrusion set to target and compromise French entities. This activity is part of a long-running campaign, active since at least 2004, aimed at intelligence gathering from strategic entities and individuals worldwide, including within France. Specific French victims include government ministries and organizations in the diplomatic, defense, justice, and technology sectors. These espionage campaigns are particularly notable in the context of Russia's war of aggression, with continued targeting against Ukraine, NATO countries, and EU member states. France and the European Union have formally attributed these malicious cyber activities to the 16th Centre of the FSB.

## Impact

The observed activities lead to the compromise of critical French governmental and strategic entities for intelligence-gathering purposes. Success of these espionage campaigns results in the exfiltration of sensitive information from ministries, diplomatic missions, defense contractors, justice departments, and technology sector organizations, potentially impacting national security, foreign policy, and economic interests. The continuous nature of these campaigns against NATO and EU member states poses a significant and ongoing threat to international security and stability.
