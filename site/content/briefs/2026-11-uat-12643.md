## Overview
Cisco Talos identified UAT-12643 as a financially motivated threat group utilizing the Go-based Overlord remote access trojan (RAT), the Python-based 'Good Game' infostealer, and a multi-cryptocurrency clipboard hijacker. Talos assesses with low confidence that the actor is North Korea-aligned based on TTP overlaps with known actors. Malicious files were hosted on a server at 41.216.188.11, running a Flask-based file delivery service on port 8000.

## Attack Chain
The actor employs trojanized installers for applications such as Viber and MicroSIP to deliver payloads. The Overlord RAT utilizes Solana blockchain transaction memos as a C2 dead-drop channel. Payloads include mechanisms to harvest documents with filenames in English, French, Spanish, and Portuguese.

## Impact
The actor targets financial and accounting staff, messaging platform users, VoIP softphone operators, and cryptocurrency and gaming users. The infostealer's language-based harvesting capability targets documents in English, French, Spanish, and Portuguese.

## Recommendation
- Monitor or block traffic to the infrastructure at 41.216.188.11.
- Monitor network traffic for interactions involving Solana blockchain transactions utilized for C2.
- Monitor for the execution of trojanized installers masquerading as legitimate software.
