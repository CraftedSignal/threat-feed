## CVE-2026-86950

An out-of-bounds write vulnerability in the CoreGraphics component of Apple's iOS, macOS, and iPadOS platforms allows for potential arbitrary code execution. The flaw is identified as CVE-2026-86950 and is currently tracked by CISA as a known exploited vulnerability, necessitating urgent patching to mitigate the risk of exploitation.

Affected products:
- iOS
- macOS
- iPadOS
