OpenClaw versions before 2026.3.12 are vulnerable to credential exposure. The vulnerability stems from the embedding of long-lived shared gateway credentials directly into pairing setup codes. These codes are generated by the `/pair` endpoint and the `OpenClaw qr` command. An attacker who obtains these setup codes through various means, such as leaked chat histories, logs, or screenshots, can extract the embedded credentials.
