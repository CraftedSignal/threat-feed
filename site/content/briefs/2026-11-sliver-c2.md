A new Sliver command-and-control (C2) server has been identified at 93.152.221.150 on port 31337. Sliver is a cross-platform adversary emulation and penetration testing framework commonly leveraged by threat actors for beaconing, lateral movement, and modular payload deployment.

## Impact
Presence of this indicator suggests active C2 infrastructure capable of executing post-exploitation tasks, maintaining persistence, and facilitating data exfiltration within compromised environments.

## Recommendation
- Block inbound and outbound traffic to 93.152.221.150:31337 at the network perimeter.
- Review historical logs for connections to this IP address to identify potential prior compromise.
