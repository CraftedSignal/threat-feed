A server-side request forgery (SSRF) vulnerability has been identified in DataLinkDC Dinky up to 1.2.5. The vulnerability exists in the `SqlGateway.onOpen` function within `SqlGateway.java` of the `sql-gateway` component. Manipulation of the `connectAddress` argument leads to server-side request forgery. The attack can be launched remotely. Public exploit material is available.
